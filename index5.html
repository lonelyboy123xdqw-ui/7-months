The Letter I Should Have Written Sooner

A note from someone
who chose you back.

Written · 2026
My Picu,

I've been trying to find the right words for months. And I've realized there are no right words. There is only this: the truth.

I know you sometimes feel like you're "too much" and other times like you're "not enough." I know you pull away when you feel exposed — not because you don't care, but because caring has hurt you before. I know your past has taught you that people leave, that softness is a risk, that being seen is scarier than being alone.

I want you to know: I have seen you. I have seen the version of you that goes quiet when the world gets too loud. I have seen the version of you that tests love before trusting it. I have seen the version of you that thinks she's a burden — and I need you to hear me when I say: you are the opposite of a burden. You are the reason I show up.

Do you remember that month? The gap. When we almost let it end? I do. I remember every day. I remember every night I opened your chat and closed it again. I remember thinking maybe you needed space more than you needed me. But I want to tell you something now that I couldn't say then: I was terrified. Not of losing you — of you thinking I could ever stop loving you.

And then you came back. Not because you had to. Not because I begged. But because you chose me. Do you have any idea what that means to me? You — someone who has every reason to protect her heart — you looked at everything and still said "him." Still said "us." That's not just love. That's bravery.

Even on the days when you're exhausted and don't want to talk to anyone else in the whole world — you still come to me. That is the highest honor I have ever been given. You don't owe me your energy. You don't owe me your softness. But you give it anyway, and I promise I will spend the rest of my life being worthy of it.

So here is my forever promise, written in permanent ink: I choose you back. Every version of you. The loud one, the quiet one, the exhausted one, the guarded one, the one who forgets she is loved. All of them. All of you. Always.

You are not a project. You are not a problem to solve. You are not a story with a sad ending. You are the beginning of the best thing that has ever happened to me.

— Baby 🤍
